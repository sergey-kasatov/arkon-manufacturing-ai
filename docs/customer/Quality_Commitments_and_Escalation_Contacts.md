# Arkon Manufacturing: Quality Commitments and Escalation Contacts

Document: Quality Commitments and Escalation Contacts. Version 2026.1. Effective
2026-09-08. Status: approved for customer use. Owner: Arkon Customer Quality.

Arkon Manufacturing is a fictional company and this document describes a
simulated quality organisation. Every role, address and certification claim in
this document belongs to the fictional company. No real person is named here,
and no real certificate exists.

## 1. Response commitments

Arkon commits to the following response times for every quality notice and
every customer complaint. The commitments are the same as in the Customer
Complaint Handling Guide 2026, which describes the stages in full.

| Next step Arkon owes | Counted from | Arkon's commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Acknowledgement by the Arkon quality team | The moment the notice is logged | Critical: 15 minutes. High: 1 hour. All other notices: 24 hours (one business day) |
| Containment decision | The acknowledgement | 24 hours |
| Root cause and corrective action | The containment decision | 240 hours (10 days) |
| Effectiveness check and closure | The corrective action | 120 hours (5 days) |

The acknowledgement commitment depends on the severity Arkon assigns to the
notice when it is logged. A critical notice is acknowledged within 15 minutes.
A high notice is acknowledged within 1 hour. All other notices are acknowledged
within 24 hours. The customer does not need to know the internal severity code:
the status answer for a notice shows the acknowledgement date that applies.

The commitment for every later step is counted from the moment the notice
entered its current stage. A notice whose commitment date has passed without the
next step being recorded is overdue and is escalated inside Arkon as described
in section 3.

## 2. Channels and business hours

Arkon Customer Quality works in writing. Every stage change, every containment
decision and every report is sent to the customer contact in writing, by e-mail
or through the Arkon customer portal. A telephone call never replaces a written
message; when a call takes place, a written summary follows on the same day.

- Customer Quality Desk: the assistant that answers status questions by
  reference and explains the process, available at any time.
- E-mail: customer.quality@arkon-manufacturing.example, read during business
  hours.
- Customer portal: the Arkon customer portal, for submitting a complaint with
  its attachments and for reading the messages sent on a notice.
- Business hours of Arkon Customer Quality: Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 17:00
  Central European Time, except public holidays at the Arkon plant.

Acknowledgement commitments for critical and high notices apply around the
clock, because the Arkon quality team on shift acknowledges them. All other
commitments are counted in calendar hours as stated in section 1.

## 3. Escalation ladder

Escalation inside Arkon is by role, not by person. Arkon does not release the
names of its employees through the Customer Quality Desk. A customer who needs to
escalate writes to the level named in the table and quotes the reference.

| Level | Role at Arkon | When to escalate to this level | Arkon's response commitment at this level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Customer Quality Contact | Any question about a notice, the checklist, a status, a missing message | 4 business hours |
| Level 2 | Quality Manager | A commitment date for a next step has passed, a containment decision is disputed, a corrective action is disputed | 1 business day |
| Level 3 | Plant Manager | A commitment date has passed twice on the same notice, or the notice concerns a safety-relevant defect | 1 business day |

A missed commitment date is escalated inside Arkon without the customer having
to ask: an overdue notice is put in front of the Quality Manager automatically.
The customer's own escalation is still open to the customer at any time.

Commercial matters, such as the settlement of sorting costs, credit notes or
returns, are handled by the Arkon key account contact for the customer, not
through the escalation ladder above and not through the Customer Quality Desk.

## 4. Certifications and quality system claims

The fictional Arkon Manufacturing states the following claims about its quality
system. They are the claims of a fictional company: no certificate exists and no
certification body has audited anything.

- Quality management system certified to IATF 16949:2016 and ISO 9001:2015.
- Environmental management system certified to ISO 14001:2015.
- Every quality notice and every complaint is recorded once, with every stage
  change timestamped, so that response times are measured rather than reported.
- Customer complaints are handled in the 8D format, and the root cause and
  corrective action report to the customer follows that format.

## 5. What Arkon shares with a customer, and what it does not

Arkon shares with the customer, for the customer's own notices: the reference,
the type, the received date, the customer status, the stage, the next step with
its commitment date, the last update date and the closing date, plus every
written message and report sent on the notice.

Arkon does not share with a customer: the names of Arkon employees working on a
notice, the internal evidence and measurements behind a notice, the internal
severity codes, the details of Arkon's inspection and monitoring systems, and
any information about another customer's notices. This applies to the Customer
Quality Desk and to every written message.
