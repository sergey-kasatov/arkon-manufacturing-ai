# Arkon Manufacturing: Customer Complaint Handling Guide 2025

Document: Customer Complaint Handling Guide. Version 2025.2. Effective 2025-03-01.
Status: SUPERSEDED on 2026-09-08 by the Customer Complaint Handling Guide 2026,
version 2026.1. This version is kept for the record and must not be used.

Arkon Manufacturing is a fictional company and this guide describes a simulated
quality process.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This guide explains how Arkon handles a customer complaint about parts Arkon has
delivered. It tells the customer what happens at each stage, what Arkon commits
to, and what Arkon asks the customer to do.

## 2. The reference

Every complaint receives one Arkon complaint number. The Arkon complaint number
has the format CQ-2025-####, for example CQ-2025-0117. The complaint number is
assigned by the Arkon customer quality office within two working days of the
complaint arriving. Quote the complaint number in every message about the
complaint.

## 3. The four stages of a complaint

Every complaint moves through four stages. The stage is shown to the customer as
a stage name.

| Stage | Stage name | Next step Arkon owes | Arkon's commitment for the next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open | Written acknowledgement by the customer quality office | 48 hours |
| 2 | In progress | Containment decision | 5 working days from the acknowledgement |
| 3 | Resolved | Corrective action report | 30 working days from the containment decision |
| 4 | Closed | None | Closure letter within 10 working days after the corrective action report |

### Stage 1: Open

At stage 1 the complaint has arrived at Arkon. The stage name at stage 1 is Open.
The next step Arkon owes at stage 1 is the written acknowledgement by the
customer quality office. Arkon's commitment for the written acknowledgement is
48 hours from the complaint arriving. Every complaint is acknowledged within 48
hours regardless of its severity.

### Stage 2: In progress

At stage 2 the complaint has been acknowledged and is being investigated. The
stage name at stage 2 is In progress. The next step Arkon owes at stage 2 is the
containment decision. Arkon's commitment for the containment decision is 5
working days from the acknowledgement.

### Stage 3: Resolved

At stage 3 the containment decision has been taken and the root cause analysis
is under way. The stage name at stage 3 is Resolved. The next step Arkon owes at
stage 3 is the corrective action report. Arkon's commitment for the corrective
action report is 30 working days from the containment decision.

### Stage 4: Closed

At stage 4 the complaint is closed. The stage name at stage 4 is Closed. Arkon
sends the closure letter within 10 working days after the corrective action
report. A closed complaint can be reopened by the customer within 90 days by
quoting the complaint number.

## 4. What the customer receives

The customer receives the written acknowledgement, the containment decision, the
corrective action report and the closure letter by e-mail from the customer
quality office. Telephone calls are used for urgent complaints, and the caller's
name and telephone number are recorded on the complaint.

## 5. Checking the status of a complaint

The customer asks the customer quality office by e-mail or telephone, quoting the
complaint number. The customer quality office answers within 48 hours with the
stage name, the name of the Arkon engineer handling the complaint and the
engineer's telephone number.

## 6. What Arkon asks of the customer

- Report the complaint with the part number, the delivery note number, the
  quantity affected and a description of the defect.
- Quarantine suspect parts until the containment decision.
- Return samples of the defective parts at Arkon's cost when asked.

## 7. Commercial settlement

Sorting costs, rework costs and credit notes are settled by the Arkon customer
quality office together with the closure letter. Arkon accepts the customer's
documented sorting costs up to the value of the affected delivery.
